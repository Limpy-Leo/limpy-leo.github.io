Les Tres

Match 3D Puzzle. Support and help.

Les Tres is a 3D matching puzzle. Objects are poured onto a board as real physical things, and you tap three of a kind to clear them into the tray. The pile behaves like a pile: things settle, slide, and bury each other.

Common questions

h2>
How do I play?
Tap any object to send it to the tray at the bottom. Collect three of the same kind and they clear. Clear the whole board before the clock runs out. If the tray fills with no match available, the level ends.
What are the boosters?
Hint clears a triple for you, Undo throws the last object back, Shuffle heaves the pile over, and Freeze stops the clock. Tap an empty booster to restock it with coins.
What are the pre-game boosts?
Armed before you press play: an extra tray slot, a head start that removes nine objects, or a hot start that begins your streak at five.
What is Club Tres?
An optional weekly membership. It doubles what a win pays and the daily reward, gives five free continues a day, unlocks Zen mode, and adds two free replays of the daily ranked board.
What is Zen mode?
Endless boards with no clock and no streak. Nothing to lose and nothing counting down. Included with Club Tres.
How do the weekly and monthly campaigns work?
Each is a ladder of stages that unlock over the period, with a free lane and an optional paid lane. Earlier stages stay open, so a missed day can be caught up. Everyone plays the same boards.
I lost my purchases.
Tap Restore Purchases on the home screen. Coins, boosters, and campaign passes are tied to the Apple ID that bought them.
What does Les Tres require?
iOS 17 or later.
