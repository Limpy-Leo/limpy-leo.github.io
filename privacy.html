Limpy Leo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This policy applies to all Limpy Leo apps, including Peeks, Rota and I Am.

Limpy Leo apps do not collect any personal data.

Our apps have no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Where an app works entirely offline, it makes no network requests at all.

Information stored on your device

Anything an app needs to remember — your progress, statistics, settings, alarms, and similar — is stored only on your device. This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is not accessible to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting an app removes its data.

App permissions

Our apps request only the permissions they need to function, and never to collect information:

Peeks
Requests no permissions.
I Am
Requests no permissions. I Am works entirely offline and makes no network requests at all.
Rota
Requests permission to schedule alarms and send notifications, used solely to make your alarms ring at the times you set.

Purchases

Some apps (for example, Peeks) offer in-app purchases such as a one-time unlock or optional packs. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Our apps never see or store your payment details, your Apple Account, or any other personal information — they only ask Apple whether this device is entitled to a purchase, so they know what to unlock.

Third parties

Limpy Leo apps include no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

Limpy Leo apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@limpyleo.com.
